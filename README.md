# Data Analyst Internship – Task 2

## Data Visualization and Storytelling

This project was completed as part of the Data Analyst Internship Task 2. The objective was to create visualizations that communicate a clear business story using sales data.

## Tools Used

* Power BI
* CSV Dataset
* GitHub

## Dataset

The project uses the **Sample Superstore** sales dataset containing sales, profit, quantity, product, category, region, and order date information.

## Dashboard

The Power BI dashboard includes:

* Total Sales KPI
* Total Profit KPI
* Total Quantity KPI
* Sales by Product Category
* Sales by Region
* Profit by Product Category
* Monthly Sales Trend
* Top 10 Products by Sales
* Key Business Insights

## Key Business Insights

* Technology leads in sales and profit.
* West region records the highest sales.
* Furniture has the lowest profit among the three categories.
* Sales show an overall upward trend in the later years.

The dashboard demonstrates the use of appropriate charts, KPI cards, visual storytelling, and business insights to communicate sales performance effectively.
